# Decision sheet B: Go-to-Market and forecast

Opus pre-fills from the launch data, the thesis and Sheet A. Steve edits once. Output: launch plan, private label section, and forecast.json, which the forecast runner turns into the workbook, the dashboard and the download in one command.

Format per line: Question | Default (Opus) | Basis | Steve

## Launch plan
1. Playbook (distributor-first, anchor-first, club-first)
2. Tier 1 anchors and why
3. Tier 2 and 3 unlocks (accounts, distributor, gate)
4. Hold list and the reason for each
5. Wave 2 lineup and order

## Private label
6. Firewall: what is reserved for private label (format, product)
7. Current programs (retailer, product, status)
8. Targets ranked, with pitch and recommendation per target

## Forecast (becomes forecast.json)
9. Frame: calendar year or fiscal year, and which year
10. Existing business: accounts, doors, SKUs, SRP, velocity (units per store per week)
11. New format velocity vs existing (default equal; raise if higher perceived value or multiple usage)
12. Wave 2 velocity
13. Launch timing: first drop month and SKU count, later drops, when other retailers can start
14. Existing-account expansion: skips the review wait (default yes)
15. Hit rate by tier (anchors, unlocks), and per account overrides
16. Ramp curve (default 40 / 70 / 100 percent at months 1 to 3, 4 to 6, 7+)
17. Trade and marketing percent by tier, existing line and new items. Always on gross sales, never shelf dollars
18. COGS per unit by format (default margin calculator ceilings, flagged)
19. Waterfall: retailer and distributor margin (default 40 / 15)
20. Dashboard tiles: revenue basis, net label (default Contribution Margin), percent tile (default Contribution Margin percent of gross sales)

## Standard outputs (not decisions)
- P&L block on Overall: retail at shelf, gross sales, trade, net sales, COGS, gross profit, marketing, contribution margin, each as percent of gross sales
- Tabs: Read Me, Assumptions, Overall, Current State, existing account detail, Retailer Expansion, SKU Expansion, account Plan and Vol templates
- Forecast section tabs: Forecast and Budget dashboard, Model tabs, Retail Marketing (shared slide)

## v85 amendments (2026-11-10)
- Steve answers by line number, changes only. An unanswered line means the default stands.
- Anything answered at intake is pre-filled as confirm only, never asked twice.
- Line 14: the review-wait skip applies only where the client is already an approved vendor; category reset windows still apply unless the client has a commitment.
- Line 17: where blank, total trade plus marketing defaults near 20 percent of gross sales, split by tier.
- Lines 10 to 12: existing items use the client's real velocity; the hold-low rule applies only to a brand with no store history.
